> This guide covers Mythic GME 1.7.0 and later.

Lists keep track of the people, goals, and hazards driving your adventure, and they double as random tables when a Random Event hits. Every Journal in the app keeps its own set of lists for **Threads**, **Characters**, and **Features**, plus any **Custom Lists** you add.

## What each list holds

- **Threads.** Your Player Character's goals, missions, quests, and open tasks. You use this list in every game.
- **Characters.** Active NPCs along with other forces that can shape events, including factions, places, objects, or ongoing situations. You use this list in every game.
- **Features.** Unique elements from a prepared or published adventure that can turn into encounters or twists. Use this list only when playing a prepared adventure.
- **Custom Lists.** Extra lists you name yourself, available with Expanded Features.

When a Random Event points to Threads, Characters, or Features, the app rolls on that list and drops the result right into your Adventure Log.

## Start a new adventure

Before your first scene, add a few starting entries so Random Events have something concrete to pull from:

1. Add your main **Threads**, such as "Find the lost artifact".
2. Add your key **Characters**, such as "Mysterious sage" or an active place like "Haunted manor".

Keep entry names short and specific. You can write longer descriptions in your scene notes whenever you need them.

## Update and weight lists between scenes

Most list edits happen during end-of-scene Bookkeeping, so your lists reflect what just happened in play. Random Events always read your latest list state right away.

- **Weight important entries.** If a Thread or Character played a big role in the last scene, tap the duplicate button on its row to copy it into the next open slot. Extra copies raise its odds of coming up in future Random Events. By default, the app stops at 3 copies per entry. To allow more, turn off **Keep list entry copies limited to 3** in **List Settings**.
- **Remove stale entries.** Delete Threads that are finished or abandoned, and remove Characters that are no longer part of the story.
- **Clean up gaps.** Use **CLEAN UP** to close empty gaps between rows while keeping all of your duplicate copies as they are.
- **Condense heavy weighting.** **CONDENSE LIST** is a separate control that closes empty gaps and trims duplicate weighting at the same time. Any entry listed three or more times drops to two copies, and any entry listed twice drops to one.

## How list sections and rolls work

Each list holds up to 5 sections of 5 slots. Tap the plus button below the slots to add a section, and tap the minus button to remove the last section.

When the app rolls on a list, it follows the same section-and-line method as a paper Mythic list. It looks only at sections that hold at least one entry, picks one of those active sections at random if more than one has entries, and then picks one of that section's 5 slots at random.

If the roll lands on an empty slot, the app returns **Choose**, and you pick whichever entry fits best. If you want the app to always land on a filled slot instead, turn on **Never Roll "Choose"** in **List Settings**. The app will then reroll its own list rolls until it hits a filled line, as long as the list has at least one entry.

## Use the Features list for prepared adventures

When you run a published or prepared adventure, fill in the **Features** tab right next to **Threads** and **Characters**. Use it for adventure-specific triggers that can show up during play, such as wandering monsters, staged visions, villain hit-and-run tactics, or poison and disease progressions.

A Feature can overlap with your Characters list. For instance, "Vampire Lord attacks" can live on Features while "Vampire Lord" sits on Characters.

In prepared play, switch to the **Prepared Adventure Event Focus Table** instead of the standard Event Focus table. When a Random Event rolls **Adventure Feature**, the app rolls your Features list. If **Auto Roll Event Meaning** is turned on (it is off by default), the app also rolls a pair of Action Meaning words even when a Features entry comes up, so you can lean on those words whenever the Feature needs extra detail. For the full setup, read [Setting Up a Prepared Adventure in the Mythic GME Apps](/resources/setting-up-a-prepared-adventure/).

## Build Custom Lists

Custom Lists are part of Expanded Features. A Custom List is a list you name yourself inside the open Journal, sitting right alongside Threads, Characters, and Features. The Mythic rules recommend extra lists for long campaigns, such as one Characters list per region or one Threads list per Player Character.

To create a Custom List:

1. Open **Lists** and tap the **+** tab. On a wider layout, use the **+** destination or column instead.
2. Tap **Add Custom List**.
3. Type a name and tap **Save**.

On a phone, the new list gets its own tab. In the wider Lists layout, it gets its own column. Names cannot be blank or match another Custom List, and **Characters**, **Threads**, and **Features** are reserved.

Under **Starter lists**, you can also pick **PC NPCs** (people tied to your Player Character) and **PC Threads** (your Player Character's personal goals). The **Personal Event Focus Table** can roll on both. Tap a starter list to create it empty right away. Each starter option appears only while you do not already have a matching list.

A Custom List has the same sections, slots, roll button, notes, and copy button as the built-in lists, with a few differences to watch for:

- **Removing a section.** The minus button deletes the last section and any entries in it. If that section has entries, the app asks you to confirm first, and you cannot undo it.
- **Clearing entries.** **CLEAR ALL** empties the slots while keeping your notes, and you can undo it.
- **Copying.** **Copy All** skips Custom Lists. Use the copy button on the individual Custom List instead.
- **Renaming or deleting.** Tap **More options** on the list to choose **Edit** or **Delete**. Deleting a Custom List cannot be undone.

An entry in a Custom Table can also link to a Custom List. That link matches by list name, and renaming the list does not update the link automatically. If you rename a linked Custom List, update the table link to match.
