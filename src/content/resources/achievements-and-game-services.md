> This guide describes Mythic GME 1.7.1 and later.

Mythic GME can award achievements in Game Center and Google Play Games as you play. There are 25 achievements. Game services work only on Android, iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Turn on Game Services

Game Services is off by default. The app signs in to Google Play Games or Game Center only after you turn it on.

- **On a phone:** open Settings, then the **Game Behavior** tab, then **Game Services**, and turn on **Enable Game Services**.
- **On a tablet or computer:** open Settings, scroll to the **Game Services** section, and turn on **Enable Game Services**.

Achievements are awarded only after sign-in to Google Play Games or Game Center succeeds. Turn the switch off at any time to stop the app from signing in.

## See your achievements

While **Enable Game Services** is on, a **View Achievements** key sits under the switch. Tap it to open the Google Play Games achievements screen on Android, or Game Center on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The app signs you in first if it needs to.

If the screen can't open, the app shows this message: "The app could not open your achievements. Check that you are signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, then try again."

## How progress counts

Many achievements look at the Journal you have open. When you open a Journal that already qualifies, for example one where the Chaos Factor is 9 or one with 10 Characters on its Characters list, the achievement unlocks then, so play from before the update counts.

On Android, progress toward **Ten Scenes Deep**, **Fifty Scenes**, **Chronicle** and **Epic Saga** counts only new Scenes and Adventure Log entries you add after you sign in, during the same session. **Scene One** uses the current Scene count, so an existing Scene counts toward it after you sign in. In Game Center, progress uses the current counts in the open Journal.

## The 25 achievements

### Oracle and Random Events

| Achievement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **Exceptional Yes!** | Roll your first Exceptional Yes on a Fate Question. |
| **Exceptional No!** | Roll your first Exceptional No on a Fate Question. |
| **Twist of Fate** | Trigger your first Random Event during a Fate roll. |
| **Interrupted!** | Get your first Interrupt Scene from a Scene Check. |
| **Two Dice, One Fate** | Answer a Fate Question with the Fate Check. |
| **Following the Trail** | Make a Discovery Check. |

### Chaos Factor

| Achievement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **Off the Rails** | Let the Chaos Factor climb all the way to 9. |
| **Steady Hand** | Bring the Chaos Factor all the way down to 1. |

### Scenes, Threads and Characters

| Achievement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **Scene One** | Create your very first Scene in an adventure. |
| **Ten Scenes Deep** | Create 10 Scenes across your adventures. |
| **Fifty Scenes** | Create 50 Scenes across your adventures. |
| **Thread's End** | Play the Conclusion of a Thread Progress Track. A Conclusion counts whether or not the Thread is resolved. |
| **Full Cast** | Have 10 Characters on one Journal's Characters list. |
| **Waiting in the Wings** | Trigger a Keyed Scene you set up. |

### Writing and returning to your story

| Achievement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **In Your Own Words** | Write Narrative entries in 5 different Scenes of one Journal. |
| **Chronicle** | Reach 100 Adventure Log entries in a single Journal. |
| **Epic Saga** | Reach 500 Adventure Log entries in a single Journal. |
| **Back at the Table** | Play one Journal on 10 different days. The days don't need to be in a row. |
| **Many Moons** | Play one Journal on 30 different days. The days don't need to be in a row. |

### Hidden achievements

The other 6 achievements are hidden. Game Center and Google Play Games show their names and how to earn them only after you unlock them, so this page leaves them as a surprise.
