During game prep and live play, the pieces you need rarely sit in one place. You might need a dungeon map from an adventure book, two monster stat blocks from a bestiary, and a condition chart from the core rulebook. Clipping lets you pull sections or whole pages from multiple books onto one **Table**, a visual reference board you can lay out however makes sense for you and your players.

## How books connect to a Table

Every Table pairs with the books you are using:

- Books inside an open **Set** all clip onto that Set's shared Table. This is how you pull rules, monsters, and maps from up to 8 different books onto one board.
- A standalone book opened outside a Set clips onto its own Table.

You can open any Table from **My Tables** on Home, where each board previews as a framed mini-board. While reading, tap the scroll button in the upper-right corner of the Viewer to open or create the Table linked to your current book or Set.

## Clip a section or a whole page

While reading any book, open **Tools > Clip** and choose how much to capture:

- **Select area** lets you drag a rectangle around a single stat block, table, map section, or spell description.
- **Whole page** captures the entire page in one step.

Switch to another book in your Set and clip more pieces as you read. Each new clip queues into the right-edge **Clips** tray on your Table, so you can keep reading and lay out the board when you are ready.

## Place and arrange clips on your Table

Open the Table and pull open the right-edge **Clips** tray. Drag a clip onto the board, using a long press on touch screens, or tap **Place**.

Once a clip is on the board, drag to move or resize it, tighten its edges with **Crop item**, bundle related pieces with **Group**, or freeze it in place with **Lock item** so you do not bump your layout mid-session.

## Jump back to the exact source page

A clip is never detached from its book. Select any clip and tap **Open in PDF**, or press `Enter` on desktop, to jump straight back to that exact page in the source book. When you want to return to your reading session from anywhere on the board, use **Open book** or **Open set** in the Table corner.

## Keep clips working after Update PDF

If a publisher releases errata and you replace the file with **Update PDF**, your existing clips remain visible on the Table. Open the clip's menu to **Resync** it with the updated pages, or choose **Remap** if the page numbers shifted.
