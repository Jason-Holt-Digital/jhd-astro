Keeping your tabletop library in one place lets you browse adventures on the couch and grab the right rulebooks when a session starts. Bring books in from your device or DriveThruRPG, tag your shelf, and group each campaign into a Set.

## Import local PDFs

Open **Import > Local PDF > Choose PDF** to import one book or select several PDFs at once. Tome Keeper stores its own copy of each file, so your original PDFs stay untouched.

You can also hand PDFs straight to Tome Keeper from other apps. Use the Share Sheet or Open In on iPhone, iPad, and Android. On Mac, use Open With or drag a PDF onto the Tome Keeper icon in the Dock.

## Import from DriveThruRPG

Open **Import > DriveThruRPG** and paste your **Application Key**. If you need to create a key, tap **Where do I find my key?** and turn on **My Library Access** on your DriveThruRPG account.

Large DriveThruRPG libraries load page by page so you can browse without waiting for the entire catalog to finish. If a slow page times out, tap **Retry** while keeping everything that already loaded. Use these controls to narrow your list and download titles:

- **Hide imported** hides books already in your Tome Keeper library.
- **Show partial matches** surfaces editions or bundles that closely match your search.
- **Allow re-import** lets you pull down a fresh copy of a book you imported before.
- **Import selected** downloads every checked title into **My Books**.
- **Disconnect** removes your stored Application Key from that device.

## Organize your shelf with Library Tags

Open the menu on any book in **My Books** to keep your shelf tidy. Use **Edit title & tags** to rename a book or assign **Library Tags** for systems, settings, or campaigns. Use **Update PDF** to swap in a revised errata edition without losing your bookmarks, tags, or Table clips. Use **Remove from library** when you want a book off your shelf.

Once you tag your collection, pick any tag in **My Books** to filter the shelf down to that system or campaign.

## Group campaign books into Sets

A **Set** holds up to 8 books together for one campaign or game system. Open a Set and all of its books open together, ready for reading, cross-book search, and clipping onto one shared Table.

## Switch between open books

Open books appear as tabs along the top **Book Strip**. Reveal the strip in four ways:

1. Swipe down near the top edge on touch screens.
2. Hover your pointer at the top edge on desktop.
3. Open **Tools > Open Books**.
4. Press `Command-Shift-B` on Mac.

Drag tabs to reorder them, or tap **+** to add another book. For fast one-step switching, hover or press and hold the lower-left open books stack on the page control to fan out your open covers.

When multiple books are open inside an active Set, choosing **Remove from Set** on a tab removes that book from the saved Set. Closing the last open book ends your reading session and leaves the saved Set intact.
