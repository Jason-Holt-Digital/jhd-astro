When you read an RPG book for fun, you want the page clean. When a player asks how grappling works in the middle of combat, you want that exact paragraph right away. Bookmarks, Page Tags, and cross-book Search give you fast lookup without cluttering the page.

## Jump by contents or page number

Open **Tools > Contents** to browse the book's table of contents and jump straight to a chapter. To flip by page number, drag the lower-left page scrubber or open **Go to page** and enter the exact page you want.

## Save pages with Bookmarks and Bookmark Edge Tabs

Open **Tools > Bookmark** to mark the page you are reading. Pick from 8 preset categories. Your choices are **Combat**, **Spells**, **Items**, **Monsters**, **NPCs**, **Maps**, **Rules**, and **Custom**. You can also enter a custom `#RRGGBB` hex color if your group uses its own color code.

Every bookmark appears as a vertical **Bookmark Edge Tab** along the edge of the page, like a sticky tab poking out of a hardcover book. Tap any tab to jump straight to that page. To review every marked page in the book or filter by category, open **Tools > Bookmarks**.

## Pin Page Tags onto maps and stat blocks

Sometimes you want to mark one spot on a page instead of the whole page. Open **Tools > Tags** to create reusable **Page Tags** with your own label, icon, and color.

Drag a Page Tag right onto a dungeon room, hex tile, encounter table, or stat block. Page Tags stay anchored to that spot on the page, so you can label secret doors, treasure rooms, or house rules without covering up the artwork.

## Search one book or an entire Set

Open **Tools > Search** to search inside the current book. If you have a Set open, switch scope to search across every book in the Set at once.

Tome Keeper always ranks search results in a fixed order:

1. **Bookmarks**
2. **Page Tags**
3. **Contents**
4. **PDF text**

Because your own Bookmarks and Page Tags appear above raw text matches, the rules and map locations you already flagged come up first instead of getting buried behind dozens of stray mentions.

## Current search and markup limits

Search indexes bookmarks, Page Tags, the table of contents, and embedded PDF text. Scanned image-only PDFs are not OCR-searchable unless the PDF already contains a text layer.

Tome Keeper also leaves book pages clean and does not include PDF ink drawing or text highlighters. When you want working notes alongside a rule or map, place a Page Tag on the page or clip that section onto a Table.
