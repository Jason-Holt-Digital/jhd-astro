Book clips give you the rules, and Table components hold everything else you need during a session. Put your prep notes, trackers, character sheets, handouts, and dice right beside your clipped pages.

## Add notes, tools, and shapes

Open the right-edge **Components** tray on any Table to drop items onto the board:

- **Notes.** Place a **Sticky note** for quick reminders, NPC quirks, or loot, or place **Text** for clean headings across your layout.
- **Table tools.** Use a **Frame** to group an encounter or location, a **Counter** to track hit points, torches, or progress clocks, and a **Checklist** for session prep, spell slots, or active conditions.
- **Shapes.** Choose from 9 shapes, including live-drawn **Line** and **Arrow** connectors that rotate freely to link clues, dungeon rooms, or faction relationships.

## Import images and fillable PDF character sheets

Open **Components > Import** to bring outside material right onto the board.

Choose **Image** to add maps, NPC portraits, or player handouts from Files, Photos, or Paste.

Choose **PDF** to place a character sheet or handout on the Table, with each page appearing as its own board item. If the PDF already includes form fields, switch to **Fill mode** to type directly into the sheet. If you import a flat PDF without form fields, choose **Add text fields** to place writable fields where you need them. When you want to share or back up a character sheet, tap **Export filled PDF** to save a filled copy without changing the original file.

## Navigate large Tables

When a campaign board grows wide, open **Navigator** to see the whole layout at once and jump straight to any corner. Use the zoom presets for `50%`, `100%`, `200%`, and **Fit to content** to switch between close reading and a full overview.

Table does not include an undo action yet. Once a section of your layout is set, use **Lock item** on key clips, frames, or sheets so you do not shift them by accident during play.

## Roll up to 12 physical 3D dice

You can roll dice without leaving your book or your Table. Tap the `d20` button in either the Viewer or a Table to open the **Dice** tray.

Tap the die keys for `d4`, `d6`, `d8`, `d10`, `d12`, `d20`, and `d100` to build a pool of up to 12 physical 3D dice at a time. Each `d100` uses 2 slots because it rolls both tens and ones dice. Tap any die inside the tray to remove it from your pool.

Throw the pool to watch physical 3D dice roll on screen, and open **History** whenever you want to check earlier rolls.
