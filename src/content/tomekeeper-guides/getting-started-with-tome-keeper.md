Tabletop RPG books are full of art, two-page spreads, and stories worth enjoying on their own. Tome Keeper opens them like real books without utilitarian PDF toolbars crowding the screen, while keeping your whole library ready when you switch to game prep or table play.

## Add your first rulebook

To bring a PDF onto your shelf, open **Import > Local PDF > Choose PDF** and pick one or several files. Tome Keeper keeps its own copy inside the app, so your original files stay untouched where you left them.

You can also send a PDF straight into Tome Keeper from other apps. Use the Share Sheet or Open In on iPhone, iPad, and Android, or use Open With or drop a PDF onto the Dock icon on Mac. Tap any cover in **My Books** to start reading.

## Read with controls out of your way

When a book opens, the page fills the screen. Reading controls stay tucked inside **Tools** in the lower-right corner until you tap it. A quiet page scrubber sits in the lower-left corner when you want to skim through pages.

If you prefer having your top controls visible across every book on that device, open **Tools > View** and turn on **Keep top bar shown**.

## Choose single pages or open spreads

Open **Tools > View** to choose how pages sit on screen:

- **Single** shows one page at a time. Zoom out to see it framed as a single page leaf.
- **Spread** pairs facing pages side by side with a faint fold crease down the center. Zoom out to see the open hardcover book frame around your spread.
- **Show first page as cover** keeps page 1 centered as a closed hardcover cover when you read in **Spread** view.
- **Lock layout** holds your chosen page layout steady while you flip through the book or rotate your device.

## Customize how your books and shelf look

Open **Settings > VIEWER > Book look** to pick the backdrop around your pages. Choose **App bar linen**, **Desk**, **Quiet outline**, or **Off**. Right below that option, toggle **Show page scrubber** if you want an even quieter screen.

Open **Settings > APPEARANCE** to tune the rest of the app to your taste:

- **Theme.** Pick **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**.
- **Bookcloth colors.** Pick from 7 bookcloth colors. Your choices are **Reference Desk**, **Oxblood**, **Ink Blue**, **Plum**, **Teal**, **Copper**, and **Charcoal**.
- **Book binding.** Pick **Linen**, **Smooth**, or **Quarter cloth** for how book spines look on your shelf.
- **App icons.** On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android, pick from 7 matching woven bookcloth app icons. Windows uses the default icon.

Once your first few books look the way you want, group a campaign's books into a Set or clip key pages onto a Table when it is time to run a session.
