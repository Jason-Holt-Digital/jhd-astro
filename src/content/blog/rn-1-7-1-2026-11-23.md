Mythic GME 1.7.1 is rolling out now to Mythic GME Mobile and Mythic GME Digital. It brings back Clear and Remove in Dice Studio, explains how Dice Studio and dice mode work together, adds 15 achievements, and makes sure an update never replaces a built-in table you changed.

## Clear and Remove in Dice Studio

**Clear**, at the top of the Dice Studio roll list, empties the list after you confirm. **Remove**, or a swipe to the left, takes one roll off the list. Studio test rolls are deleted. Rolls made in dice mode stay in your Journal and the Adventure Log.

## Dice Studio and dice mode

The first time you open Dice Studio, a short sheet explains it. Dice Studio is for building, testing and saving dice formulas, and its rolls are never written to your Journal. To roll dice into the Adventure Log, switch the Fate Chart screen to dice mode. The sheet shows where that key is.

Dice Studio also lists your dice mode rolls. To list only Studio test rolls, turn off **Show Adventure Log rolls** in Dice Studio settings.

[Dice Formulas & Customization](/resources/dice-formulas-and-customization/#dice-studio-and-dice-mode) covers the roll list, the intro sheet and the new setting.

## Achievements

Earn 25 achievements in Game Center and Google Play Games, 15 of them new. Turn on **Game Services** in Settings, then tap **View Achievements** to see them. Achievements work on Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Most of the new achievements look at the Journal you have open, so play from before the update counts once you open that Journal. [Achievements and Game Services](/resources/achievements-and-game-services/) lists every achievement and how to earn it.

## Your table edits stay yours

The app never replaces a built-in table you changed. A table you edited in the app keeps your edits through every update.

If you changed a table's file in your storage folder and an update changes that table, Table Management in Settings lists it under **Tables Waiting for Your Choice**. Until you choose **Keep Mine** or **Use the New Version**, a dot marks the way into Settings. A copy that can't be read waits there too, and play uses the app's version until you choose. Random Events keep working while a table waits.

[Getting Started with Custom Tables](/resources/getting-started-with-custom-tables/#when-an-update-changes-a-built-in-table) explains each choice.

## Fixes

- On Linux with NVIDIA graphics, the app fills its window again instead of drawing in one corner of a black window.
- The Linux Flatpak now uses the Freedesktop 26.08 runtime, which still gets security updates.
- On iPhone and iPad with a Screen Time passcode, the app no longer asks for the passcode and an age range at every launch.
- **Use the App's Version** in Table Management works again.
- A roll made just as the app comes back from the background stays in the Adventure Log.
- Changing the storage folder no longer fails while it moves your saved formulas.
- On Mac, a storage folder you choose stays usable after you restart the app.
- Dice Studio opens in a very small panel instead of failing.
- With Focus Mode on, the navigation rail icons on tablets and desktop show in dark mode.
- Scene summaries use the same font and line breaks as the Adventure Log.
- A primary key, such as **New Scene**, shows a clear ring when it has keyboard focus.

## Help, tables and translations

- Meaning Tables search finds a table by its name in your language, and Traditional Chinese shows Traditional category names.
- In Campaign Elements, the Crucial Characters entry now says Crucial Character where it said Running Influence. The slip came from Mythic Magazine 63.
- The Savage Worlds example in Dice Help shows a full trait roll with a Wild Die, `(1d8!! + 1d6!!)kh1`.
- The 3D dice Help says Sample roll works when your device reduces motion.
- German and French have a shorter Saved label in the dice bar, so it fits on small phones.
- Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and German say the export-all sheet bundles files instead of packing them, and the Portuguese Suspect Descriptors 45 reads Cheio Grande.
