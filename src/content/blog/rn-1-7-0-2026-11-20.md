Mythic GME 1.7.0 is out now across Mythic GME Mobile and Mythic GME Digital. This update brings several features players have asked for over the past year, along with a cleaner ruled-row look that makes long sessions easier to scan on phones, tablets, and desktop.

## Journals and Custom Lists

Your open Journal is now visible right at the top of the Adventure Log on every device. Tap the Journal title to see all your Journals, switch to another campaign, or start a new one. On tablets and desktop, Journals opens in a centered window. The side menu also opens on a card for the current Journal over a night sky banner with Switch and New Journal buttons, and tablets and desktop put those same actions in the side navigation. If you are starting fresh with an empty Adventure Log, a one-time welcome card explains how Journals work.

Custom Lists are now part of Expanded Features (available through the one-time unlock on mobile and included in Mythic GME Digital). You can create your own named lists inside a Journal alongside Threads, Characters, and Features. Open the `+` tab for one-tap `PC NPCs` and `PC Threads` starter lists. Personal Event Focus results for PC NPC and PC Thread now roll directly on those lists, and Custom Table entries can link to and roll any Custom List by name.

Regular List rolls now follow the Mythic GME 2e rulebook. The last section with an entry sets the section die, so empty sections above it can still come up while empty sections below it are skipped. List settings such as Never Roll "Choose" and the copy limit now live behind the Lists settings button. A Journal can also remove the Features list and bring it back later under **Show Features list** in List Settings without losing any saved entries, unless a Prepared or Published Adventure Event Focus table is active. On wide screens, Lists adds a **One list / All lists** switch to view every list side by side.

## Dice Studio and 3D dice

The standalone dice roller is now named **Dice Studio**, and its Operators tab is now **Join**. You can also switch between Fate and dice mode right from the Fate screen using shared trays, edit a formula inside its pill, and log general dice rolls straight into the Adventure Log.

- **Labeled colored dice pools.** Save labels like `Attack` and `Damage` on the Join tab and tap them into a formula. One formula can roll multiple labeled pools at once, such as `"Attack": 1d20+5, "Damage": 2d6`. Each pool shows its own total. Assign one of six quick colors or a custom color to each label, and a color dot marks its line in both Dice Studio and the Adventure Log.
- **Push, Year Zero Engine, Forbidden Lands, and Ironsworn.** Dice Studio now supports Year Zero Engine d6 pools that count 6s as successes and 1s as failures, with `@push` and `@lock` formula tags (for example `5d6#s6 @push=1`). Forbidden Lands Push keeps both 6s and 1s, and you can push a roll directly from Dice Studio or the Adventure Log. Labeled pools also let you roll mixed dice together, such as an Ironsworn action roll and its challenge dice.
- **Step-by-step formula breakdown.** Dice rolls now show each die, modifier, and number as a key. By default, **Steps** works through the formula in order on numbered lines. Dropped dice are struck through, and exploded dice show `!`. Under **Dice roll display** in Settings, you can also switch to **Full breakdown** (one line per part) or **As typed**.
- **3D dice with sound and physics.** Turn on **Animate dice rolls** to throw 3D dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100, and D66) for Fate rolls, table rolls, and dice formulas across mobile, desktop, and web. Dice make collision sounds against the table, screen edges, and each other. Drag from the Roll button in Fate, dice mode, or Dice Studio to aim and throw them, or pick up and toss any die on the table, even after its result is recorded. Moving a die after its result is recorded never changes the logged result. Exploding, compounding, penetrating, and rerolled dice launch extra 3D dice in follow-up throws while earlier dice stay on the table, and Exceptional answers, Random Events, and Interrupt Scenes finish in slow motion.
- **New 3D Dice Panel.** 3D dice controls moved from Settings into a dedicated **3D dice** panel inside Dice Studio. Under **Motion**, tune Gravity, Throw, Spin, Bounce, and Friction. Under **Dice**, set Linger starting from half a second, choose **Basic** dice with **Gloss** from Matte up to 100% or the new **Retro** set with its barrel d4, and color each die individually. If glossy dice draw slowly on your device, a note suggests Matte.

## New Mythic Magazine tables, Progress Points, and translations

Expanded Features adds 30 new tables from Mythic Magazine issues 63 through 71, including 29 Meaning Tables and 1 Event Focus Table (`Node Random Event Focus Table`). That brings the built-in library to 250 Meaning Tables and 16 Event Focus Tables. You can now hide any built-in tables and categories you do not use and bring them back anytime from Table Settings.

Slide open any Meaning Table or Custom Table row to favorite it, choose a 1x or 2x roll, or tap **+PP** to set its Progress Points (from 0 to 99) for the open Journal. The 13 progression tables from Event Crafter, Emerging Powers, and Hello Focus roll `1d10 + PP` or `1d100 + PP` and display `roll + PP = total`. Progress Points work with both 3D dice and your own physical dice in Hybrid Play.

Every built-in table now ships translated in five more languages (German, Spanish, Italian, French, and Chinese), and built-in table names are in Portuguese too. The full app, including Help articles and What's New, is now localized in Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. European Portuguese now uses European Portuguese terms such as `Definições` and `Guardar`.

## A fresh look, panels, and offline Help

The Adventure Log, Lists, Scenes, Meaning Tables, Event Focus, Keyed Scenes, and Progress Tracks now use a ruled-row ledger layout. Text is one step larger across the app, and section labels follow your device's text size. On the Target Numbers bar, a sliding needle marks the newest Fate Chart roll with a short glow and sparkles on Exceptional Yes and Exceptional No, while Fate Check highlights the Modifier total.

Tablets, unfolded foldables, and desktop get the same **Game Tables Pull-Out** from phones. Meaning Tables, Event Focus, Progress Tracks, and Keyed Scenes open from tabs on the right edge of the Fate screen, and you can turn the pull-out off in Settings under Layout. On wide screens, Meaning Tables also lists categories beside the tables.

On tablets and desktop, **Edit Panels** now shows every panel slot. Drag a panel to move or swap it, use **Move to**, pick from four starting layouts on the Layout card, and undo any change. The Classic 6-Panel desktop layout is retired. If you used it before, the app opens once on the **Tables, Log, Lists** layout. If you never changed your standard tablet or desktop layout, Meaning Tables moves into the pull-out while **Scenes, Adventure Log, and Lists** sit side by side. Any custom panel layout you already saved stays as you left it.

Other screen and tool updates in 1.7.0:

- **Writing cards and Scene separators.** Add Narrative, list notes, and the Scene editor open as writing cards with basic formatting, Write and Preview tabs, and undo and redo. Starting a new Scene adds a Scene separator row to the Adventure Log with its title and Chaos Factor, and the Scene editor lets you insert those log entries straight into the Scene Summary. **Copy All** behind the gear button on the Scenes screen copies every Scene Summary in order.
- **Dark Mode.** Dark Mode colors are fixed across all 10 themes.
- **18 new offline Help articles.** Offline Help now groups articles by topic, including Getting Started, Chaos Factor, Random Events, Custom Lists, Dice Studio, and Troubleshooting. Each screen's Help button links directly to the articles for that screen, every Help sheet and article shares one header and text style, and Settings descriptions are shorter.
- **Date and Time settings.** Choose Automatic, Day/Month/Year, Month/Day/Year, or Year-Month-Day for dates, and Automatic, 24-hour, or 12-hour for times, right beside Language in Settings.

## Apple Watch, Wear OS, storage, privacy, and fixes

Both the Apple Watch and Wear OS companion apps update to version 1.7.0. Random Events on both watches now use the Mythic GME 2e Event Focus and Action Meaning tables instead of the first edition tables. Both apps now use a black background, larger touch targets, the d10 icon, and neutral Yes and No colors with blue and orange reserved for Exceptional results. Each watch app runs on its own without requiring phone sync.

On Android, **Choose a Folder** in Settings lets you store Journals, formulas, and tables in a folder you pick, such as `Documents/Mythic GME`, where the Files app can see them. On Apple devices with iCloud sync enabled, Meaning Tables and Event Focus open faster, and if iCloud offloads a table copy from your device, the app downloads it in the background instead of freezing.

Privacy controls are clearer in 1.7.0. On first launch after updating, the app asks once about error reports, Basic counts, and Usage sharing. Error reports never include your Journal content and can be turned off anytime. Basic counts and Usage sharing stay off unless you turn them on in Settings, and Session Replay has been removed.

Stability fixes in 1.7.0:

- The Mac build on itch.io now opens cleanly instead of staying on a black or loading window.
- Saving no longer creates false `"Conflicted copy"` files, including when saving pasted text with blank lines or Windows line breaks.
- Fixed a dice sound crash when many dice hit at once.
- With iCloud sync on, the first Random Event after an update no longer fails to open your data.
- Long screen titles fit without cutting off, side navigation labels no longer split words across lines, Custom Tables phone tabs no longer flash a plain list, the Scene Actions menu stays on screen on tablet and desktop, and light mode keeps Lists tabs and the Odds and dice trays visible.
- Restore Backup for Saved Formulas now checks each backup file before restoring and lets you know when none can be restored.

## Good to know when updating

A few defaults changed in 1.7.0 so the app matches the rulebook out of the box.

- **Auto Roll Event Meaning starts off.** A Random Event rolls the Event Focus and lets you tap the Meaning Table that fits your scene. Turn **Auto Roll Event Meaning** back on in Settings under **Show Random Event Button** if you want the app to roll Action Meaning automatically.
- **Keyed Scenes default to your next Scene.** Triggered Keyed Scenes now mark the event for your next Scene by default. Switch back to **Newest existing Scene** behind the gear button in the Keyed Scenes panel if you prefer the earlier placement.
- **Progress Tracks end by the rules.** The "How a Progress Track ends" setting now defaults to **By the rules**, so a track finishes after its Conclusion whether or not the Thread was resolved. Choose **Open-ended** behind the gear button in the Tracks panel to keep tracks open until you tap Thread Resolved.
- **1.6.8 Journal compatibility.** Version 1.6.8 can still open a Journal saved in 1.7.0, unless you start a new Scene, roll in dice mode, or use other 1.7.0-only features in that Journal.
